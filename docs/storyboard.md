# Storyboard: editing it (for the agent)

The storyboard (`/storyboard`, `components/Storyboard.jsx`) is the user's board of the videos'
frames. What the user changes on it lives apart from the captured frames, in
`content/storyboard/<video>.json` (and `trash.json` for the Trash). This page is how to change it
when the user asks.

## The rule: change only what was asked — DO NOT CHANGE HOW IT LOOKS

When the user asks for an edit on the storyboard ("retype frame 3 of Date & time", "make that
text red", "move the bubble up", "add a line called …"):

- Change **exactly** that, with `scripts/storyboard.mjs` (below). Never rewrite the edits file by
  hand, never recapture (`gen:storyboard`), never touch the demos' CSS or components for it, never
  "tidy" other frames, positions, sizes, colors or fonts.
- Look first (`frame`, `texts`), then change, then look again to confirm. Say what changed in the
  user's words (line name + frame number, as the board shows them).
- An open storyboard page shows the change within ~3 seconds (it polls the file's revision) and
  keeps the user's own unsaved edits (it merges); the user can Ctrl+Z it on the page.
- If the user's request is about the look of the whole board or the video itself, that is not a
  storyboard edit: ask.

## The command line

```
node scripts/storyboard.mjs help                  # every command
node scripts/storyboard.mjs lines                 # lines: id, name, number of frames
node scripts/storyboard.mjs frames "date"         # frames of a line, named <line>:<n> as numbered on the board
node scripts/storyboard.mjs frame "date:3"        # its note, its assets (key, name, box), what was changed
node scripts/storyboard.mjs texts "date:3" bubble # the texts of an asset, with their keys
node scripts/storyboard.mjs find "Knowledge Base" # frames whose label, note or texts say it
```

`--video increased-efficiency-v2` for the other video (`improved-quality-v2` is the default,
`trash` the Trash). A frame is `<line>:<n>` (the line by id, name, or the start of its name; `n` as
the board numbers it, from 1 in each line) or its id. An asset is its key (or its name); a part of
one is the key `texts` prints (`report/0.0.1.2`, `report/0.0.1#1` = a piece of text beside others).

## Files, revisions, backups

- `content/storyboard/<video>.json` holds the edits (`lib/storyboardEdits.mjs` documents every
  field) and `rev`, bumped on every save. A save made from an older revision is refused (409) and
  the page merges and saves again, so the page and the command line never overwrite each other
  (`lib/storyboardStore.mjs`, `/api/storyboard`).
- Backups: `content/storyboard/backups/<video>/<time>--<reason>.json`. Always before an import or
  a restore, at most one every 10 minutes while saving, the newest 80 kept. The sidebar lists them
  (nothing picked → Backups → Restore) and `/api/storyboard/backups` serves them.
- Export (sidebar or `export`): every board and the Trash in one file
  (`{mark: 'taskmaverick-storyboard-export', version, exportedAt, boards}`, `makeExport` /
  `readExport` in `lib/storyboardEdits.mjs`), editable: Import puts it back. Without the file
  (production), the page downloads the storyboard as it was before it imports.
- **A reload keeps your place (the user, 2026-10-13: "when I refresh the website using f5 or ctrl+shift+r or any way, I wanna be where I was before, when I refresh it takes me up"):** the board
  is a canvas, not a scrolled page, so the browser has no position to give back and it used to open on the first row every time. The view is kept in `localStorage`
  (`storyboard:view:<video>`, one per board and the Trash) as the board point in the middle of the window plus the zoom (`keepView` / `restoreView` in `lib/storyboardView.mjs`: the same
  place is in the middle of a window of any size), each time a move settles and on `pagehide` / `visibilitychange` (a reload in the 130 ms before a move has settled keeps where it was
  heading). The board opens on it (`opened` effect in `components/Storyboard.jsx`); it is only ignored when it is not usable or its middle is nowhere near the board (a line deleted:
  the bounds are every frame and label, NOT the rows, which are only the labels' width), then the first row as before. The other long pages scroll the window, and the browser already
  returns to the same spot after F5 and Ctrl+Shift+R (checked: the landing page, both libraries, `/improved-quality-v2`).

## Chat boxes: 300 wide, not locked (the user, 2026-10-06; unlocked 2026-10-07)

Every chat box on the boards is **300 wide (stage px), not locked** (2026-10-07: the lock was removed by default, on both boards; the sidebar's lock still locks one); a new one (Shift+A)
starts that way. A chat box given a width wraps its words to it: as captured they flow as one text
(the demo's own lines were made for its width), words retyped keep their own lines, and a box made
narrower sends its words to the next line (the font never changes). Its height follows its words
(`sizes` [w, 0]) unless one was given. After `npm run gen:storyboard` the new frames' boxes are not
sized yet: `node scripts/storyboard.mjs bubbles 300` (and `--video increased-efficiency-v2`).

## Chat boxes centered vertically (the user, 2026-10-06)

Every chat box on the storyboard sits with its vertical middle on the frame's (y = 450 of 900); only
its height position changed (its x is where the user put it). A box's middle is the middle of its words
(`bubbleBoxOf`), so it was measured in the browser (every frame drawn live, the words' middle in stage
px) and moved by the difference with `moves`: 85 boxes on Improved Quality (all lines but "The tablet and
its Knowledge Base", which the user had just reviewed and keeps as placed) and 36 on Increased
Efficiency. A new line of frames, a recapture or a retyped box (it grows) needs it again.

## Background designs (the user, 2026-10-07: "not changing just colors, I need different styles")

A background can be a DESIGN, not a color: an `add-html` asset 1600 x 900 at 0,0 (inline SVG / CSS gradients only), sent `layer ... back`, over the frame's own background paint. Ten were made for the Scratch line (styles 15-24): dot grid (a dot field masked to thicken round the words), blueprint (40 / 200 px grid + cross marks), aurora (big blurred orbs), diagonal split (SVG polygons), topography (seeded noisy contour rings), signal rings (concentric circles with nodes), hex mesh (a honeycomb fading away from the words), waves (layered paths), pinstripe (a repeating 135-degree gradient + a beam), light rays (blurred polygons fanning from the top). Each has the inset panel (`r2-rectangle` at 28,28, 1170 x 844, radius 40: frosted glass on dark, white card with a shadow on light), a typeface, the text 360 wide and an accent bar 96 x 4 placed 30 px under the measured text. Replace one design: `hide <frame> <asset>`, `add-html` again, `layer ... back`.

## Handwriting: words written by a pen (the user, 2026-10-07: "literally handwritten, not word by word, not line by line")

A chat box can have an animation, `anims.<bubble key> = {type: 'handwrite', cps}` (letters a second: the sidebar's **Speed** slider, 4 to 60, default 22; older ones hold `wps` or `seconds`) on its frame (sidebar: **Handwriting**, `animate` on the command line; copied by a duplicate and by Ctrl+C / V). It lives **in the slide itself**: a frame with one shows a small **blue round play button with a white triangle at its top right** while it is selected (a white square while playing; Esc or the button stops it). Playing writes the words with a pen along the strokes of the letters. `lib/storyboardHandwriting.mjs` (pure, tested) thins the text's pixels to a center line (Zhang-Suen), splits it into lines of text, then into pieces that touch (a letter, or a whole word in a joined hand), and walks them like a hand: line after line, piece after piece left to right, a stem from its top down, the dot of an i and the bar of a t after their letter; `components/storyboardHandwriting.js` draws the words on a canvas in their own font, shows them through a pen-thick mask the pen paints, with a small nib at its tip, then puts the page's own words back (the frame never changes). The words are hidden only while the pen writes. Not in the storyboard video player (yet).

The **Fonts** line (above Scratch in `improved-quality-v2`, `line-add Fonts --first`): the same slide in 14 handwriting faces (Caveat, Kalam, Patrick Hand, Indie Flower, Shadows Into Light, Reenie Beanie, Dancing Script, Permanent Marker, Homemade Apple, Nanum Pen Script, Gloria Hallelujah, Covered By Your Grace, Zeyada, Sacramento), each with its handwriting animation and a note naming the font. Fonts are `next/font/google` instances in `app/layout.jsx` (`preload: false`) and ids in `FONTS` (`lib/storyboardEdits.mjs`) / `FONT_STACK`, `FONT_MATCH` (`components/storyboardDom.js`).

## Font size and line height (the user, 2026-10-07: "I change the font size and it just spreads the lines apart")

A text's line height is automatic (1.3 x its size) until someone sets it; a set one is a fixed px number. Changing the font size in the sidebar now moves a set line height with it (`scaledLineHeight`), and a number field only saves when its value was really changed (clicking into Line height and out again used to save the number it showed, which then stayed put while the size changed). The one frame that had such a frozen value (`links/bubble`, 45.49 at 34.99, identical to automatic) was set back to automatic.

## No empty chat boxes: they show the start of their words (the user, 2026-10-06)

A frame of "Bubble, no words" (the box popped, the words not yet typed) never shows an empty yellow
box: it shows the first half of the first line of the words that will come (3 words: the first 2; 2
words: the first 1), followed by empty lines, so the box keeps the size of all its words and looks like
mid-typing. It is `bubbles.words = true` plus a retyped text (`typing`); the numbers of lines were
measured from the box itself (`[data-sb-mode="empty"]`, its wrapped words at 300 wide). A new frame of
that kind, or a recapture's, needs it again.

## Captured frames never show a click half done (the user, 2026-10-06)

The frame "After the click" is taken when what the click did is finished: `planChapter`
(`lib/storyboardFrames.mjs`) waits for the stop's `settle` (seconds) when it has one, else 0.55 s after
it. Micro-training's Submit folds the quiz and the video into a Done tile, which takes longer than that
(the frame once showed it 13% open, the hand cut off under the card): its stop has
`settle: MICRO.done + .1`. Give any new click whose effect is slow a `settle`.

## No tails on the storyboard (the user, 2026-10-06)

Every chat box on the storyboard has its tail taken off (`tails off`; per box: `frames[id].bubbles`,
`{tail: false}`; the sidebar's Chat box section turns it back on). The frames that only showed a tail
("Tail", "Hand coming out, tail halfway") and the "Tail fading" frames that then looked like the one
before were moved to the Trash (24 on Improved Quality); frames with a note, or made / edited by the
user, were kept. The videos themselves still have their tails (that is the demos' rule in CLAUDE.md).
To dim a tablet's board under an open panel, use its own shade: `style <frame> tablet/0.0.0.0.1
opacity=0.5` (`.mi-demo-shade`, the layer the demos dim with).

**The video is not the slides one after the other (the user, 2026-10-08): "don't worry about the slides, just make me a good video".** The player's
director (`directorOf` in `components/storyboardPlayerDom.js`) turns the slides of a line into the script of the video; the slides are never changed:
- **Slides that are not played:** one that only holds the start of the next one's words (`isTypingStep`: "As Teams", "Teams Are"), one that shows the
  tablet, a title or the words half faded (a layer's `opacity` under 1: a step of a fade), one that looks like the one before. The video never shows
  incomplete or faded text.
- **Words:** a chat box's words are always written by the pen (the chalkboard hand), at the chat box's Speed (sidebar Handwriting, words a second),
  else 22 letters a second (a little faster than 3 words a second; the user, 2026-10-09). Words that stay from one slide to the next are not written again. A **title and the conclusion** fade in small and grow (see below); one that comes after the art waits until the art has gone out (tablet out,
  the Panel wiping across), then it comes in.
- **Highlights and what is drawn over the art** (a Highlight, the timeline of a video, a ripple: `isOverlay`) come in only after the art they point at
  is in place (`LATE`: a moment after the move ends, then a short fade), and go out first; they never glide. The frame stays on long enough to see them.
- **The cursor** (the Hand layer; the user, 2026-10-09) is ONE cursor, on screen from its first click to the end of the scene (it fades out slowly with the
  scene change). It comes in once from the top left of the words, goes to the **exact middle of what it clicks** (`centerHand`; what is clicked is the word the slide makes bold, else what it highlights, else what is under its cursor on the slide; checked: Menu button, Knowledge Base, Onboarding and the video are each 0 to 1 px from the fingertip; the words themselves when the
  clicked button / menu row / card has one text, else the thing's middle; the menu is the button labelled Menu, not the plus; a video's play button is the middle
  of `.iq2-kb-film`), hovers, clicks (it presses, a ring spreads, THEN the screen changes), and goes **straight on to the next thing to click**: menu →
  Knowledge Base → Onboarding → the video's play button. A *click* is where the slide has the cursor when the tablet changes in the next slide
  (`sources` in `directorOf`); the slides in between (the same tablet, only the cursor and a highlight moving) are not played (`dropped`), the frame after a
  click already has the cursor at the next thing. The cursor is never crossfaded with another one (that was the flicker). **It is always on top of the tablet that changes** (a frame is drawn over the one before: the cursor that glides is the NEW frame's, the old one waits out of sight, and the ring spreads over the new tablet; the old way, the cursor under the fading-in tablet, looked like the cursor fading out before the click). Checked by pixels (yellow in the cursor's box at every 0.06 s). Every move takes 0.6 s (`STD_MOVE`; a click move `CLICK_TOTAL` 1.15 s: the cursor stays on what it clicks, going along with the tablet as it glides, presses, the screen changes, and only THEN does the cursor go on; 
  a click move 1.4 times that, the cursor waits for the press), a hover 0.3 s; holds are short (`HOLD`: 0.4 to 1.4 s).
- **Hover:** the text under the cursor is as it is in the software until the cursor arrives; then it turns bold with a text stroke, smoothly, and **does not
  move** (what the slide did to it - bolder, bigger, nudged - is stripped from the slide, `emph`, and only tells which text it is). **Click:** the cursor
  presses, a ring spreads, then the cursor fades out and the screen changes.
- **After a title** the next scene is the one with its text (a tablet by itself in the middle is not shown). **Words that a slide shows while the cursor is still
  at work** (Knowledge Is Readily Accessible, on the slide where the drawer opens) are written after the click that opens what they are about (Onboarding), and
  the dark panel stays away until then. **A title and the conclusion** fade in small and grow; the first title fades out growing when the tablet comes, the conclusion stays on (it does not fade out at the end) (`TITLE`); the slides that show the
  title smaller or fainter (steps of that) are not played, the video does it.
- **Highlights** wait for the cursor to have arrived and be resting on them, then fade in (`LATE`); one that the slide draws earlier is moved to that frame.
- **The video's timeline** (the layer named Video timeline) is put exactly on the bottom of the film (its width, its bottom edge) and runs by the clock (0:00 up).
  The film is a real `<video>`: loaded as soon as the frame before it is built, it **plays by itself** while the video plays (set right only when it drifts
  by 0.4 s; set by the clock when the video is scrubbed), and is paused when its frame is left. The words that came after the Onboarding click stay (with the
  panel and the tablet beside them) while the video plays; the frame stays on for 4.5 s of it. The cursor stays on the film and leaves with the tablet at the
  scene change (it does not fade on its own).
- **Centering:** when the right side is gone (the Panel parked off the slide, `away`) the tablet and what is on it go to the middle of the video
  (`centerArt`) and glide to the left again when words come.
- **Speed (the user, 2026-10-09):** the pen's Speed slider is in **letters** a second (4 to 60; `anims` hold `cps`; older `wps` is read as 6 letters a word; an older `seconds` was only the old default, the video does not follow it), because words are not all as long;
  `node scripts/storyboard.mjs animate <frame> <chat box> handwrite [letters/s]`.
- The move animations are made when a move starts (a paused one holds its first keyframe and would override the fades the frames do by their clock).

**A good video of the software (the user, 2026-10-09/10: "scroll animation in the software, expanding to show mission details in a smooth way ... check every frame").**
- **The software moves, it is not crossfaded.** Two slides of the same screen (same tree, 3 levels deep: `morphable`) are one screen in two states: every
  element goes from its old inline values to its new ones (`morph`, `MORPH_PROPS`: transform, scale, opacity, visibility, height, width, top, left, maxHeight,
  gridTemplateRows, marginTop, clipPath, and the screen's own place) — the checklist scrolls, the chip opens into Mission Details, the quiz folds into its
  Done tile, the knob slides. A card the demo names (`data-card`, `data-mission-id`, `data-iq-target`: `MOVE_KEYS`) that is somewhere else in the next slide
  **glides there** (`glideFrom`): a claimed mission goes from Open to Claimed, the priority card rises to the top and the others slide down. Cards of one board
  laid out another way glide too (`magicMove`). Something put in the place of another (a picture by the video it took) is just swapped. The software shown by
  another piece altogether (the phones, then the tablet; overlap under 0.6 or another class) goes, then the other comes (`other`, `artSwap`, `artLeaves`).
- **Clicks, from the demo's own names.** The slides are named after the hand's stops (`lib/storyboardFrames.mjs`: "Hand on X (k of n)", "Click X (k of n)",
  "After the click (k of n)"). `clickTarget` (`lib/demoTargets.mjs`) gives the stop of the click (HAND_STOPS of the story) and `targetSelector` its element
  (the demos' TARGETS, now shared from `lib/demoTargets.mjs`; the steps' one spot is the n-th Yes): the cursor goes exactly there. A ripple's slide that still
  shows the screen before the click (a photo, a video come a moment later) is not played: the click gives the slide after it at once. "After the click" with
  no slide of its own click (the No before Add video) was made from the slide before. A "Click" of another stop than the slide before's: its change came from
  that one (the knob drag), this click's own comes after. A list still scrolling under a still cursor is not a click (`clicked`).
- **The cursor:** a click that ends its work fades in the next slide, drawn over the new screen (`handGone`); when the screen goes on by itself before the next
  click (the lesson plays, the quiz comes up) the cursor rests where it clicked (`rest`); a held stop **drags** (`drag`, `DRAG`): the rating knob goes with the

- **What you see never depends on where you are** (the user, 2026-10-06, after a screenshot of a blank,
  half-drawn board when zooming out): every frame that was changed is drawn live everywhere on the
  board, at any zoom, and stays drawn; only unchanged frames (their picture IS the capture) switch
  between picture and live. While the board moves it is one layer (`will-change: transform`) that keeps
  the picture it had when it started: zoomed far out from there it would have to cover the whole board
  at the old sharpness (gigabytes of graphics memory: the browser goes white), so after each factor 2 of
  zoom the layer is drawn again at the new zoom (`rasterZoom` in `Storyboard.jsx`). Frames coming
  into view are drawn a few per animation frame, nearest the middle first (`liveQueue`), so a fast
  zoom never freezes the board; the frame you are on is drawn at once.
