## The rules he ended up with (final)

1. **A button gets the hand. Anything that is not a button gets no hand.** The hand only
   presses things. Everything else is pointed at without a finger: the yellow
   circle/outline, or the bubble shooting out of the thing. ("Let's use the finger when we
   press buttons." — 8:24; "If it's a button you're going to press with the hand, if it's
   not a button you're going to use this." — 17:09)
2. **A general statement comes first, in a closed bubble (no tail), and the hand comes
   out of it.** Words, then actions: "Imagine if I'm making this presentation to you, I
   don't start clicking without telling you." (2:50). Used on Knowledge Base and Step by
   step.
3. **Pointing at a non-button: the bubble is "shot out of" the thing, the words go inside,
   then it disappears before the next one is shot out of the next thing.** The tail is
   for pointing. (16:00, 17:22)
4. **Between two things, the bubble collapses and reappears at the next thing, then says
   its words.** It does not travel or keep moving. ("Collapse here, reappear from here, say
   the words, collapse back, reappear from here…" — 10:01)
5. **The circle (yellow outline) is the pointer for text and blocks, and it must be
   darker.** (13:56)
6. **Highlight, don't darken or move.** Only darken when something is in the way
   (the tablet behind the Knowledge Base menu). Never move things around to point at them.
7. **Rhythm is the whole challenge.** "How do we make it feel like a demo… we're getting
   close here." (17:03). First perfect these motions, connect everything after (16:46).
